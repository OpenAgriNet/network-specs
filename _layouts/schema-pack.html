How this schema is composed

This pack is three layers. {{ pack.schema_name }} is the published @type and a oneOf over every leaf below. x-oan-schema-by-action names one schema per call, each a oneOf over the leaves that serve it. A leaf is one scheme's version of one call: a guard pinning scheme.code, a closed flat body, and where the call needs one a gate. Nothing is inherited, so a leaf can be read on its own.
